Strong Girl Bong-soon

Romantic Comedy • Fantasy · By Storybook Studio

Synopsis

A young woman with extraordinary strength takes a job protecting a game-company CEO while pursuing her own dream.

More Than Muscle

Her unusual ability creates both trouble and opportunity. With a supportive team and a mystery to solve, she learns that real strength also means protecting the people around her.

As the day comes to a close, the characters understand that the next chapter of life is not always written for them. Sometimes it begins with one brave decision, one honest conversation, or one person choosing to stay.
