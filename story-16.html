Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo

Historical • Romance · By Storybook Studio

Synopsis

A modern woman finds herself in a royal era and becomes entangled in the lives of princes competing for power.

A Moonlit Beginning

Thrown into a court full of ceremony and danger, she tries to protect her heart while learning the rules of a different time. Every choice changes the path ahead.

As the day comes to a close, the characters understand that the next chapter of life is not always written for them. Sometimes it begins with one brave decision, one honest conversation, or one person choosing to stay.
