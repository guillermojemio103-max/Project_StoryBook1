Synopsis

Five longtime friends balance demanding hospital work with music, family, and everyday life.

After the Shift

Between busy shifts, the friends make time to play in their band and support one another. Their patients’ stories remind them that care is built from skill and compassion.

As the day comes to a close, the characters understand that the next chapter of life is not always written for them. Sometimes it begins with one brave decision, one honest conversation, or one person choosing to stay.
