Synopsis

Students from wealthy families face expectations, rivalry, and complicated feelings at an elite school.

Beyond the Family Name

At a school where family names carry enormous weight, two students from different circumstances challenge the rules around them. Friendship and courage matter more than status.

As the day comes to a close, the characters understand that the next chapter of life is not always written for them. Sometimes it begins with one brave decision, one honest conversation, or one person choosing to stay.
