Synopsis

Two former classmates whose school documentary became popular meet again years after a difficult breakup.

The Film We Left Behind

An old film brings two people back into each other’s lives. Revisiting the past helps them understand how they changed and whether a new beginning is possible.

As the day comes to a close, the characters understand that the next chapter of life is not always written for them. Sometimes it begins with one brave decision, one honest conversation, or one person choosing to stay.
