What's Wrong with Secretary Kim?

Romantic Comedy · By Storybook Studio

Synopsis

A confident executive is surprised when his highly capable secretary decides it is time to move on.

The Resignation

After years of working together, a resignation makes a self-centered boss reconsider what he values. Office misunderstandings turn into honest conversations and unexpected affection.

As the day comes to a close, the characters understand that the next chapter of life is not always written for them. Sometimes it begins with one brave decision, one honest conversation, or one person choosing to stay.
